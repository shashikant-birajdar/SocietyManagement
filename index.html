Late payment rule

At the next cycle, late fee = prior unpaid balance × this percentage ÷ 100. Example: ₹10,000 pending at 2% adds ₹200. This is a one-cycle calculation; no daily compounding is applied.

How billing is calculated

Base charge combines occupancy charge, vehicle-segment parking rates for the member type, any existing unclassified parking slots at their saved legacy rate, and active recurring products/services. The prior unpaid amount is carried forward and the configured late percentage is applied when generating the next cycle.
